Ingrediently

Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Short version: Your confirmed pantry stays on your device. Ingrediently sends a resized photo or confirmed ingredient names to our cloud only after you enable Cloud Kitchen Assist. We do not sell personal information, show ads, or track you across apps and websites.

1. Scope

This policy describes how ClearPass Technologies, LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Ingrediently: Pantry Recipes (“Ingrediently”).

2. Information stored on your device

Ingrediently stores pantry items, review candidates, user-entered amounts, generated recipe drafts, saved recipes, weekly meal plans, privacy preferences, and a local anonymous identifier on your device using app storage. No account is required for this release.

3. Optional Cloud Kitchen Assist

Cloud Kitchen Assist is off until you enable it. When enabled:

  • A resized copy of a photo you select may be transmitted to our application server and AI-processing provider to suggest visible pantry items.
  • Confirmed ingredient names and broad food categories may be transmitted to create pantry-specific recipe drafts and seasonal recipe packs.
  • Photos and ingredient names are processed to return the requested result. Our application server does not intentionally persist those request payloads as pantry records.
  • Every detected item remains a candidate until you confirm it. Ingrediently does not determine freshness, safety, allergens, nutrition, or inventory quantity from a photo.

4. Purchases

If you view, purchase, or restore Ingrediently Plus, Apple and RevenueCat process subscription information needed to show products and maintain entitlement status. This may include an anonymous app user identifier, product identifier, purchase and renewal status, app version, and limited device or diagnostic information. Payment credentials are handled by Apple and are not provided to Ingrediently.

5. Optional diagnostics

Anonymous diagnostics are off by default. If enabled, Ingrediently may record broad product events such as a flow being completed, a privacy setting changing, a purchase outcome, or a non-sensitive error category. Pantry photos, ingredient names, amounts, recipes, dietary choices, precise location, contact information, and identity are excluded.

6. How information is used

  • Operate pantry capture, confirmation, recipe, planning, purchase, and restore features.
  • Return AI-assisted suggestions requested through Cloud Kitchen Assist.
  • Maintain subscription entitlements and respond to support requests.
  • Protect reliability and prevent abusive automated use.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not use Ingrediently data for cross-app advertising. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the requested feature, including Apple for App Store transactions, RevenueCat for subscription entitlement management, and infrastructure or AI-processing providers for optional cloud requests.

8. Retention and deletion

Local pantry, recipe, and planner data remains on your device until you remove it, reset the app, or uninstall Ingrediently. The “Empty pantry & reset local data” control removes local pantry, recipe-cache, planner, and privacy-preference data. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records according to their legal and operational requirements. Support emails are retained as reasonably necessary to answer and document the request.

9. Children

Ingrediently is a general-audience kitchen utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security and international processing

Cloud traffic uses HTTPS. Service providers may process information in locations where they operate. No system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your choices

You can disable Cloud Kitchen Assist and anonymous diagnostics at any time in Settings. You can clear local app data in Settings and manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID settings.
