How can we help?

Ingrediently is a confirmation-first pantry and recipe app. Find quick answers below or email us for personal support.

Email Ingrediently Support

Getting started

1. Capture

Take or choose a shelf, fridge, freezer, or counter photo. Cloud Kitchen Assist must be enabled before a photo leaves your device.

2. Review

Suggestions are never added automatically. Confirm, edit, reject, or mark uncertain items before they enter your pantry.

3. Cook

Recipe ideas use confirmed items and show every missing ingredient as a gap list. Always review AI-generated drafts before cooking.

Ingrediently Plus

Ingrediently Plus unlocks saved recipes, a seven-day meal board, and seasonal recipe packs generated from confirmed pantry items. Scanning, manual entry, review, the local pantry, standard recipe matching, and gap lists remain free.

To restore a purchase, open Settings → Ingrediently Plus → Restore purchases. To cancel or change a plan, use your Apple ID subscription settings. Purchases are billed and managed by Apple.

Troubleshooting

No photo suggestions

Check that Camera or Photos access is allowed in iOS Settings, enable Cloud Kitchen Assist in Ingrediently Settings, and confirm an internet connection.

No pantry recipes

Confirm at least one item, enable Cloud Kitchen Assist for pantry-specific drafts, then use Refresh Pantry Ideas.

Purchase not recognized

Use Restore purchases while signed into the Apple ID that purchased the plan. If the issue continues, email support with the app version and approximate purchase time—never send payment-card details.

Reset local data

Open Settings and choose “Empty pantry & reset local data.” This removes pantry, review queue, recipe cache, saved recipes, weekly plan, and privacy preferences from the device.

Safety

Ingrediently cannot determine food freshness, contamination, allergens, safe storage, nutrition, or doneness from a photo. Verify labels, storage history, temperature, cross-contact information, and safe cooking guidance independently.
